Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 30, 2026

PlaneWX ("we", "our", or "us") operates the AoE Finder mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our App.

Information We Collect

Location Data

With your permission, we access your device's location to show your distance from airports. This data is processed locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Anonymous Authentication

We use Firebase Authentication to create an anonymous identifier for your device. This allows you to sync your favorites list across devices. We do not collect your email, name, or any personally identifiable information.

Favorites Data

When you mark airports as favorites, this preference is stored in Firebase Firestore associated with your anonymous identifier. This data contains only airport codes (e.g., "KJFK") and no personal information.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide distance calculations from your current location to airports
  • To sync your favorites list across your devices
  • To improve the App's functionality and user experience

Data Storage and Security

Your favorites data is stored securely on Google Firebase servers in the United States. We implement appropriate security measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of your data.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google) - For anonymous authentication and data storage
  • OpenStreetMap - For map tiles (no personal data shared)

Data Retention

Anonymous user data and favorites are retained indefinitely unless you request deletion. You may delete your data by contacting us at the email below.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data by requesting a copy
  • Delete your data by contacting us
  • Opt out of location services via your device settings

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
