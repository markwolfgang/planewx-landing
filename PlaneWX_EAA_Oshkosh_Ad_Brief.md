## Creative Concepts

Two concepts to run in parallel:

- **O1 — General:** "Advanced Weather Intelligence" — evergreen, not Oshkosh-specific
- **O2 — Event:** "Flying to Oshkosh?" — event-targeted, Oshkosh context

### Messaging Philosophy: "Free" as a Barrier Remover, Not a Headline

The Oshkosh audience skews serious — instrument-rated pilots, complex aircraft owners, experienced cross-country flyers. Leading with "free" signals lightweight to this crowd and undercuts the advanced weather intelligence positioning. **"Free" should appear as a secondary trust-builder** (e.g., "$0 · No card required") to remove signup hesitation — not as the hero message. Lead with the outcome and capability. Let pilots click because of what PlaneWX *does*, then reassure them it costs nothing to try.

The O1 general concept is the exception — "Free flight weather intelligence. Forever." works as a headline for broader awareness campaigns targeting students or occasional flyers, but even there, the value lead ("flight weather intelligence") comes first.
