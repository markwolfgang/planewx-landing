Partner Messaging Sheet
Fly like it's your job.

PlaneWX is the decision support system for general aviation. It gives pilots professional-grade tools and instills professional-grade habits that make flying safer.

PlaneWX is the risk-management loop for pilots who don't have a dispatcher.

For GA pilots without a chief pilot or dispatcher, PlaneWX is the risk-management loop that brings airline-style discipline (brief, assess, decide, debrief) to every flight. Unlike EFBs, which stop at weather data, and unlike paper FRAT and minimums sheets filed away after a checkride, it keeps personal minimums live on every briefing.

Mission

Bring professional-grade tools and habits to general aviation so pilots flying on their own time, with families aboard, hold the same standard as paid pros.

Vision

GA closes the loop on every flight, and staying home is a legitimate outcome, not a failure. Desired outcome: measurably reduce GA accidents.

The Loop

Four parts. Mentor sits alongside all four.
1

Brief

WX Score

WX Score, from days out to hours out. Personal minimums front and center on every briefing.

2

Assess

FRAT

Pilot, Aircraft, enVironment, External. Opens 4 hours before departure, when you can honestly rate how you feel. Flags risk stacking when 3 or more factors are elevated, but never makes the call for you.

3

Decide

GO / NO-GO

Staying is legitimate. PlaneWX never recommends go or no-go. The pilot makes the call.

4

Debrief

Looks back at the flight and the FRAT you ran for that trip.

Mentor sits alongside all four parts of the loop. It is optional and opened by the pilot.
Personal minimums are structural. Set your personal minimums once, and every WX Score checks the forecast against your limits, not generic ones. FRAT is a chosen professional habit, and pilots can choose to require it before every flight.

How to talk about PlaneWX

Avoid
