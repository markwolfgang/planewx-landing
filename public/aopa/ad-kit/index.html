04 · Ad ops

Short instructions for AOPA

Source of truth for placement and files: /aopa/ads/SPECS.txt.

  • Placement AOPA.org ROS 300×250 Medium Rectangle, expandable to 600×250. Flight dates: Oct to Dec 2026.
  • Expand trigger and direction Hover expands after about 120 ms; mouse leave collapses. Tap or click expands in this demo. Expand direction is leftward: the collapsed unit stays anchored on its right edge, and the 5X5 panel opens to the left (300×250 → 600×250). Close with the × control. In production traffic, a click opens the clickTag URL in a new window.
  • File weights HTML5 zip 40.2 KB. Single-file HTML 37.0 KB (assets inlined). Initial load of modular unit about 27.4 KB (index.html + wordmark SVG + 5X5 panel art). Under the 150 KB initial-load target. No external requests from the unit.
  • Click URL https://www.planewx.ai/aopa via the unit clickTag (publishers may rewrite clickTag).
  • Fallback If an expandable GAM / custom template is not available, run the 300×250 JPG as a standard Medium Rectangle, or use backup.jpg from the HTML5 zip.
  • Delivery Send materials to pilot.ads@aopa.org at least 5 business days before launch.
  • Questions mark@planewx.ai

GAM notes: ad.size meta 300×250, animation capped at 15 s, 1px #BFDBFE border, z-index bands for collapsed and expanded states, visible close control when expanded. Rebuild deliverables with npm run build:aopa-ad.
