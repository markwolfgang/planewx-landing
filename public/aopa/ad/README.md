## Expand behavior

- **Default direction: right.** Expands rightward from a left-anchored 300×250 to 600×250. The PlaneWX panel stays at x=0 and never moves; the 5X5 panel fills x=300 to 600.
- **Configurable:** in `index.html`, set `CONFIG.expandDirection` to `"right"` or `"left"`. You can also pass `?expand=right` or `?expand=left`.
- **User-initiated:** expands on hover (desktop) or via click/tap when hosted with `?demo=1`. Production GAM traffic uses hover/click per the live template; the whole unit click still fires `clickTag` via `window.open(clickTag)`.
- **Close control:** visible × button while expanded (on the 5X5 panel); collapses without firing `clickTag`.
- **Collapsed size never exceeds 300×250** (`overflow: hidden` on `#ad`).
- **z-index:** collapsed `100` (within 0-4,999); expanded `50000` (within 5,000-1,999,999).
